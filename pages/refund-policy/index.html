Refund Policy

When a digital purchase may qualify for review, how to request a refund, and what happens to refunded Credits or items.

Effective July 30, 2026
On this pageScopeEligibilityUsed contentRequest processProcessingFraud & chargebacks

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of Credit Packs and other paid digital content in the Game. It does not limit any mandatory consumer rights available in your country or region.

2. When a Refund May Be Available

You may request a review if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same transaction.
  • You did not receive purchased Credits after a successful payment.
  • The purchase was unauthorized.
  • You are otherwise entitled to a refund under applicable law.

3. Used Digital Content

Except where required by law, a purchase is normally not refundable after Credits or digital items have been delivered and spent, consumed, converted into other items, or otherwise used.

If a refund is approved, the corresponding Credits, items, skins, or benefits may be removed from the Pilot account.

4. How to Request a Refund

Contact the official support channel where you obtained the Game as soon as possible. Include your Pilot email, transaction or order identifier, purchase date, amount, payment method, and a short explanation.

Never include a full card number, wallet password, security code, or other sensitive credentials.

5. Review and Processing

We may verify payment delivery and account usage before deciding a request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method when possible. Processing time depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

If a purchase was billed directly by an app store or another platform, its refund process and rules may control, and you may need to submit the request to that platform.

6. Fraud and Chargebacks

Fraudulent claims or abusive chargebacks may lead to reversal of Credits or account restrictions. If you do not recognize a charge, contact support and your payment provider promptly so the transaction can be investigated.
