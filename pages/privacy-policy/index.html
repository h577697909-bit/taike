Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Tank Battle: Neon Frontline processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective October 15, 2025
On this page Information How we use it Storage & sharing Your choices Children Security & changes Operator & contact

This Privacy Policy applies when you play the Game, create a Pilot account, use game features, or make a purchase. Tank Battle: Neon Frontline is operated by ALIEN ANTELOPE ATTIRE INC.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: Pilot name, first and last name, email address, country, and access code entered during registration.
  • Game information: progress, scores, achievements, selected stages, settings, inventory, Credits, skins, purchase records, and leaderboard results.
  • Payment information: selected Credit Pack or custom recharge amount, transaction identifier, payment status, and billing contact details needed to begin checkout.
  • Device storage: browser local storage used to remember account state, preferences, progress, and purchase delivery status.
Full card or wallet credentials are handled by the third-party payment provider and are not stored by the Game.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and save gameplay, authenticate Pilot accounts, deliver digital purchases, maintain rankings, prevent fraud or abuse, respond to support and refund requests, and improve service reliability.

3. Storage, Sharing, and Retention

Account and gameplay information is primarily stored locally in your browser. Payment-related information may be sent to the payment provider to process and verify a transaction. We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer. Locally stored information remains until you clear browser data or use an available deletion control. Transaction records may be retained by the payment provider under its own obligations.

4. Your Choices and Rights

You may update profile details in the Profile page, decline optional local storage through the cookie notice, or clear the Game's browser data through browser settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended for children below the minimum digital-consent age in their region without permission from a parent or guardian.

6. Security and Policy Changes

We use reasonable safeguards, but no local storage or internet transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised.

Submit privacy requests by email to service@battletankx.com or through the details on our Contact Us page.
