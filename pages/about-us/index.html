1. Our Company

Tank Battle: Neon Frontline is operated by ALIEN ANTELOPE ATTIRE INC. We provide and support the Game, its account features, virtual Credits, digital items, and related customer service.

2. Tank Battle: Neon Frontline

Tank Battle: Neon Frontline is an arcade-style browser game featuring staged combat, boss encounters, player progression, virtual Credits, battle gear, and cosmetic tank skins.

3. What We Provide

We operate the Game experience, maintain its player-facing features, provide purchase and account support, publish service policies, and review eligible refund or privacy requests.

4. Our Service Principles

We aim to present prices and digital content clearly, apply fair-play rules consistently, protect player information with reasonable safeguards, and provide accessible support channels for questions or problems.
